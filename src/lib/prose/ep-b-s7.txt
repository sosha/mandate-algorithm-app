### Scene Four — March 2027

The letter arrived on a Wednesday.

James was at his desk when Mei-ling came in from school, still in her uniform, holding an envelope with the University of Edinburgh crest in the top left corner. She stood in the doorway of his study for a moment, the envelope in both hands, not opening it.

"Open it," he said.

She opened it. She read the first sentence. She looked up at him.

"Well?" he said.

She put the letter on his desk so he could read it. He read the first sentence. Then he read the rest of it.

He stood up and hugged her, which surprised them both slightly — he was not a physically demonstrative man, and she had grown to match him in this, and the hug was brief and a little awkward, but it was real.

"Edinburgh," she said, into his shoulder.

"Edinburgh," he said.

He held the letter after she had gone upstairs to call her mother, who was at her sister's. He read it again, more slowly. Conditional on A-level equivalency results, which she would have. First-year accommodation guaranteed. A scholarship application form enclosed.

He put the letter on the kitchen table and went to the living room and turned on the news.
