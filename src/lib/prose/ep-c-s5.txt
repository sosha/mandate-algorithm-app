He read the Mandate Document in full. All five articles. The preamble and the closing statement. The two numbers at the end of Article Five: 87.3% and 23.7%.

He sat for a moment looking at the closing seal placeholder — APPENDIX 1, grey rectangle.

He thought about his grandfather, who had died in Taipei at eighty-nine. Who had never been back. Who had never stopped believing he would go back.

He thought about the second number. 23.7%. That was what staying looked like. That was what continuing to exist as a de facto independent island while China modernised its military and the United States reconsidered its commitments and the world moved toward the arrangement it was moving toward regardless of anyone's preference — that was the probability at the end of that road. Less than one in four.

He thought about whether he believed the first number. 87.3%. He ran his mind over the four hundred and eleven queries, the twelve inconsistencies that had all resolved, the coherence across time horizons. He thought: I have been validating this system for eight months and I have never found a gap it couldn't close. I do not know if 87.3% is the right number. I know it is the kind of number this system produces, and I have not found evidence that this system produces wrong numbers.

He opened the confirmation dialogue.
