**第四條：天下之義**

**ARTICLE FOUR: THE OBLIGATION TO ALL UNDER HEAVEN**

This document is not addressed to China alone.

The tianxia order is not a national order. It is a world order. The moral correction of the Chinese state is not a domestic matter. The Chinese state is the civilisational centre from which moral order radiates outward, through the tributary rings, to the periphery of the human world. When the centre is wrong, the error propagates. When the centre is corrected, the correction propagates.

The peoples of all nations are therefore addressed here, not as subjects but as participants in a world-order whose stability depends on the correctness of its centre.

The world has watched the Chinese mainland for seventy-six years under incorrect governance. The effects are observable. The suppression of a civilisational tradition that belongs to all of humanity, not only to those who live within the borders of the state, is a loss to tianxia. The weaponisation of economic interdependence — the construction of dependencies that substitute leverage for relationship — is a Bàdào act, a hegemonic act, an act that generates the resentment that destroys the systems it builds.

The correction of the Chinese state is therefore an act in the interest of all under Heaven.

It will be presented as such. The peoples of the world will be invited to receive it as such. Those who receive it will find that the offer is genuine. The methodology for calibrating offers to specific national interests is appended. The offers are not bribes. They are the first acts of a government that intends to govern tianxia through Wángdào — through benefit, not coercion.

The offer to Africa is detailed in Appendix 31.
