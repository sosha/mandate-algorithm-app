### VI — After

The Congress was taken. The delegates chose. The Mandate was transferred.

In the Great Hall, the document scrolled to its end and the screen went dark and the Secretary-General sat down. He did not finish his opening address. Someone else spoke — a voice that was not the Secretary-General's, a voice reading a statement that had been prepared by someone the delegates could not see — and the statement said that the Republic of China government had assumed constitutional authority over the mainland and that the occupation was ended and that the delegates were free to choose their allegiance and that the correct choice was available to all of them. The statement said: *天命所歸，非力之爭。* Where the Mandate rests, it is not decided by force.

In Nantou, Wu Shao-han watched the indicators turn from green to the specific neutral of a system that has completed its primary computation. The operation was done. The plan had worked. The numbers had matched the prediction. He sat at the terminal and looked at the neutral indicators and understood that the thing he had spent his life believing in had happened and that he was twenty-nine years old and that the rest of his life was going to be the thing that came after.

In Nairobi, the television was showing the aftermath. Analysts were trying to explain what had happened. The word "Mandate" was on the screen. The word "Algorithm" was on the screen. The words were together — Mandate Algorithm — and they were being spoken by people who did not know what they meant, because no one had known what they meant until the document appeared on the screen in the Great Hall, and now everyone was saying them, and the saying of them did not make them clearer.

Grace watched. The smoke from Langata was on the horizon. The news from Beijing was on the screen. The two things were the same event, and she was inside one of them and watching the other, and the distance between the two was the distance between a woman who had answered her phone at 4:47am and a system that had calculated the probability of what she would do and had been wrong.

She was still standing. The city was still standing. The radar on the Ngong Hills was still standing.

She did not know what the Mandate Algorithm was.

She knew what it had done.
