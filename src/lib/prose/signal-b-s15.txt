He thought: the second number is a death sentence. Twenty-three point seven percent. That is what the alternative looks like. That is what not proceeding looks like. That is the probability that the island survives, that the constitutional order survives, that everything his grandfather crossed the strait to protect survives — if no one acts.

He thought: I am twenty-nine years old and I am sitting in a server room in the mountains of Nantou County at three in the morning and I have just read the document that ends seventy-six years of waiting and I have no one to call.

He thought: the system is waiting.

He opened the confirmation dialogue.

He submitted the output for review.

He closed the terminal, picked up his access card, and walked out of the server room into the corridor. The corridor was empty. The fluorescent light hummed. Through the window at the far end, the mountains were dark, and below the mountains was the dark shape of the valley, and below the valley was the island, and around the island was the strait, and across the strait was the mainland, and on the mainland were 1.4 billion people who did not know that a machine in a mountain had just decided what would happen to them.

He stood at the window for a moment.

Then he went to find somewhere to be sick.
