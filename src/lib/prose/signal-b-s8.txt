**第二條：正名**

**ARTICLE TWO: RECTIFICATION OF NAMES**

Before action, correct naming. This is not ceremony. This is architecture. What is named incorrectly cannot be repaired. What is repaired under the wrong name becomes something other than what was intended. The following names are therefore rectified here, as a necessary precondition to all that follows.

THE ENTITY THAT HAS CALLED ITSELF
*the People's Republic of China* —
is correctly named:
**the occupying administration of the Chinese mainland**

THE PARTY THAT HAS STYLED ITSELF
*the Communist Party of China* —
is correctly named:
**the party that has held the Chinese people for seventy-six years in exchange for the promise of a future it did not deliver and cannot deliver**

THE ENTITY THAT HAS CALLED ITSELF
*the Republic of China* —
is correctly named:
**the legitimate constitutional government of the Chinese state, governing in the Free Area under the provisions of the constitution pending reunification with the constitutional territory currently under occupation**
