Eleven minutes. No name. Historical data integration. Authorised process.

She sat with the reply for longer than she usually sat with replies.

Then she classified the records as acceptable variance and continued.

She updated her working document and then, before she closed it, she added a line she had not added before:

*Source Node 7: volume growth 31x over baseline (Jan 2022 – Mar 2023). Within infrastructure spec. Source metadata restricted to L4+ clearance. No action required from infrastructure perspective.*

She looked at the line for a moment. Then she saved the document and closed it and made herself a cup of tea and stood at the kitchen window looking at the building across the street, which was dark except for two lit windows on the fourth floor and one on the seventh.
