### Scene Three — September 2023

The handover documentation took three weeks.

James was thorough with handover documentation. He had seen enough projects in his career where inadequate documentation came back years later — a client who couldn't find the maintenance records for a cooling system, a government agency that had lost the as-built drawings for a building they needed to modify — and he believed that the documentation was part of the work. You built the building and then you documented what you had built, precisely and completely, so that everyone who came after you could understand it.

The Nantou facility handover documentation ran to four hundred and twelve pages.

He sat in the temporary site office on the last day, going through the final sign-off checklist with the Ministry representative — a man in his fifties named Director Chen, who had visited the site three times in eighteen months and who wore a good suit and carried himself with the particular stillness of someone accustomed to being the most senior person in a room. Director Chen signed the documentation without reading it. He signed all forty-seven signature pages in the time it would have taken James to read the first three.

James had seen this before too. The documentation was for the record. The record was for other purposes. Director Chen already knew what the building was. The signatures were a formality.

"Excellent work," Director Chen said, returning the signed documentation. "The facility meets all requirements."

He did not specify what requirements. James did not ask which requirements he meant.

He drove back to Taichung with the documentation in the passenger seat and the particular flatness that always came at the end of a long project. Eighteen months. The building existed now. It was correct. Someone would put things in it and use it for whatever they were going to use it for and his work here was done.

Mei-ling had narrowed her university choices to three. She was seventeen. She had her mother's precision and her father's stubbornness and she was going to get in wherever she applied. He was fairly certain she was going to leave Taiwan. He was not sure how he felt about this.
