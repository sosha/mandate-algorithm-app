**肆 / FOUR:** The suppression of the legitimate constitutional government of the Chinese state. The Republic of China, established 1912, adopted a constitutional order in 1947. That constitutional order remains in force. Its territorial provisions, as defined in Article Four and upheld by the Judicial Yuan, have never been amended. The entity that calls itself the People's Republic of China has, for seventy-six years, occupied the constitutional territory of the Republic of China by force. Force is not legitimacy. Length of occupation is not legitimacy. Recognition by other states, whose own interests were served by the recognition, is not legitimacy. The Mandate of Heaven is not granted by states. It is granted by Heaven. Heaven has watched what has been built on the Chinese mainland since 1949. The assessment is complete. The Mandate has been withdrawn.
