**第一條：天命之失**

**ARTICLE ONE: THE WITHDRAWAL OF THE MANDATE**

The entity that has governed the Chinese mainland since 1 October 1949 under the name *People's Republic of China* came to power through civil war and has maintained power through the following means, which are enumerated here not as accusations but as documented facts, each individually sufficient to constitute the withdrawal of the Mandate of Heaven, and in combination constituting the most comprehensive forfeiture of legitimate authority in the recorded history of Chinese governance:

**壹 / ONE:** The destruction of traditional Chinese cultural inheritance. In the decade between 1966 and 1976, the entity directed the systematic physical destruction of temples, texts, artworks, family records, and ceremonial objects constituting the material archive of three thousand years of Chinese civilisation. The entity named this destruction the Cultural Revolution. The correct name is: the desecration of tianxia. No dynasty that has desecrated tianxia has retained the Mandate. This one is not the exception.
