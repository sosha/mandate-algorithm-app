The corridor is empty now. You are the last one here.

You have spent the past several hours with four people. A data scientist in Zhonghe who built a pipeline and filed queries and classified things as acceptable variance and continued. An architect in Taichung who built a building and went home on weekends and sat at a kitchen table with an acceptance letter and the news. An intelligence officer in Nantou who ran validation queries for eight months and read a document that was not supposed to be a document and went to find somewhere to be sick. An economist in Academia Sinica who wrote a sentence in the 47th appendix and went home to finish a literature review.

Through the glass, you have seen the room where it was decided.

The figure at the head of the table nodded.

You know what that nod means. You know more than any of them knew — more than the person who built the pipeline, more than the person who built the building, more than the person who wrote the footnote. You are the only one who has been in all four rooms.

The corridor is empty. The lift is waiting.

What do you do with what you know?
