**ARTICLE FIVE: THE OPERATIONAL SEQUENCE**

The instrument of correction is called Bǎituán.

The name is chosen deliberately. In 1940, one hundred regiments of the Chinese Communist Party's Eighth Route Army launched a coordinated offensive against Japanese occupation forces across five provinces simultaneously — a demonstration that coordinated action from many directions, properly sequenced, could achieve what no single force could achieve alone.

The irony is noted. It has been computed. It is retained.

The operational sequence is documented in full in the Bǎituán planning archive, access level BEYOND SPECIAL COMPARTMENTED. What follows here is the structural summary, included in this document because the document is the first step, and the document must contain within it the architecture of what follows, so that the architecture can be evaluated before the document is acted upon.

The sequence has seven phases. The sequence spans three years. The sequence culminates on the opening day of the 21st National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party, currently scheduled for the autumn of 2027. On that day, the 2,296 delegates of the Congress will be invited to choose the correct side. The conditions ensuring that the correct choice is the rational choice have been prepared across the seven phases. The probability of successful execution, given successful completion of all preparatory phases, is 87.3%.

The probability of Taiwan's survival under the current strategic trajectory, defined as maintenance of de facto independence for a period of twenty years from the date of this document, is 23.7%.

These two numbers are the entire argument for proceeding.
